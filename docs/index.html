IronMemo Audio Recorder

A Chrome extension that records microphone and browser tab audio locally, keeps recording when the tab is closed, and decodes the file after a Chrome crash.

All recording stays on your device. No server, no cloud, no analytics, no AI on your recordings without your separate permission.

What it does

  • Records the microphone.
  • Records the audio of a browser tab you pick, without a meeting bot.
  • Keeps recording when the tab is closed.
  • Decodes the file end-to-end after Chrome crashes or the machine loses power.
  • Lets you download each recording as a plain audio file.

What it does not do

  • It does not send anything to any server. No cloud upload. No backend.
  • It does not use your recordings to train any AI model.
  • It does not read page text, passwords, or browsing history.
  • It does not run any analytics.

Recording consent

Laws that govern the recording of conversations differ between jurisdictions. Before you record a meeting or a call, tell every participant, and get their agreement if the law you are subject to requires it. This is the user's own responsibility; the extension does not check it.

Status

Early development. The extension is not yet listed on the Chrome Web Store. Feedback is welcome via the issue tracker.
