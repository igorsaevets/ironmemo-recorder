IronMemo Audio Recorder

A Chrome extension that records microphone and browser tab audio locally, with optional cloud transcription. No bot joins the call.

All recording stays on your device. Cloud transcription is optional, per-recording, and only starts when you click Transcribe — nothing is uploaded without your explicit consent.

What it does

  • Records the microphone with a live level meter.
  • Records the audio of a browser tab you pick, without a meeting bot.
  • Keeps recording when the popup is closed.
  • Recovers recorded audio after a Chrome crash. Power-loss recovery is designed but not yet fully tested.
  • Lets you browse, play, rename, trim, and download recordings from a built-in session list.
  • Optionally transcribes a recording via IronMemo cloud — first 10 minutes free per account after e-mail verification.

What it does not do

  • It does not upload anything automatically. Cloud transcription requires a separate per-recording consent.
  • It does not use your recordings to train any AI model.
  • It does not read page text, passwords, or browsing history.
  • It does not run any analytics or crash-reporting inside the extension.

Recording consent

Laws that govern the recording of conversations differ between jurisdictions. Before you record a meeting or a call, tell every participant, and get their agreement if the law you are subject to requires it. This is the user's own responsibility; the extension does not check it.

Status

Available on the Chrome Web Store — free to install and use. Feedback is welcome via the issue tracker.
