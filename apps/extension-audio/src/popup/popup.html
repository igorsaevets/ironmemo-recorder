About IronMemo

IronMemo records microphone and tab audio locally in your browser. Files stay in the browser's storage (OPFS) on this device until you delete them from the Recordings page. IronMemo does not send anything to a server without your explicit request. The extension does not read page text, passwords or history — only the audio you choose to capture. It does not run AI on your recordings without your separate permission.

Data categories: microphone voice (Personal communications), tab audio (Website content).

By starting a recording, you confirm you have the right to record all meeting participants under the law of your jurisdiction.
