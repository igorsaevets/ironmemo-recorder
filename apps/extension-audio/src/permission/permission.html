Allow microphone access

IronMemo Recorder captures audio through a built-in invisible extension page (offscreen document). Chrome does not show the microphone permission dialog directly in an invisible page — that is why this visible page is needed. You only do this once.

After you click the button, Chrome will show its standard permission prompt. Allow it, and recording will work across every session with no further questions.

Ready to request permission.

What to do if the error came back after "Allow":

  1. Open chrome://extensions
  2. Under IronMemo click Details → Site settings
  3. Set Microphone to Allow
  4. Come back here and click "Allow microphone" again

Chrome sometimes swallows the first prompt without a dialog — a known MV3 behavior. Granting manually via site settings is the reliable fallback.
