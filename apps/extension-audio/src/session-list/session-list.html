Send this recording to IronMemo?

What is sent: the audio file of the recording you chose (the mix, or the single track), its title (date and time), the source type (meeting or dictaphone), its duration and size. Not sent: other recordings, page content, browsing history.

Where: to IronMemo servers (app.ironmemo.com) and their speech-to-text sub-processors, to produce a transcript. Your e-mail is verified with a one-time code before the first transcription: IronMemo uses your existing account for that e-mail or creates one — no password. Server copies can be deleted from IronMemo.

Cost: Your first 10 minutes of transcription are free (per account, not per recording — granted once your e-mail is verified); after that a recording needs IronMemo credits. The audio is kept on the server for a later unlock.

When: only when you click Transcribe on a recording. Nothing is uploaded automatically, and your existing recordings stay local until you choose one.

Details: Privacy Policy. After you continue, Chrome asks to let the extension access app.ironmemo.com.
